Server Rules

Keep the Outlands enjoyable for everyone.

General Rules

1. Be Respectful

Be respectful to other players. Minor disrespect or heated arguments may occur, but excessive harassment is not tolerated.

2. No Spamming

Any use of spam can result in a warning or mute.

3. No Advertising or Promoting

Any promoting will lead to a warning and/or a mute.

4. No NSFW

The only NSFW discussion allowed is in the Discord channel designated for it, and adult or NSFW content is not allowed.

5. Direct & Indirect Threats

Threats such as DDoS, death threats, doxxing, or other malicious acts are strictly prohibited.

6. No Toxicity in Any Chats

Being toxic is not tolerated. Even minor toxic behavior may result in an immediate mute and/or ban.

7. No Intentionally Crashing the Server

Intentionally crashing the server is not allowed.

8. No Begging

Don't ask people to buy you things with real life money! It's just plain annoying.

Enforcement

Admins, Owners, and Mods enforce these rules. Punishments may vary slightly depending on severity or repetition.

Agreement

By participating in this server, you agree to these rules and any future updates.
