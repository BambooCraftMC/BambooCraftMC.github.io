BambooCraft Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 • web.bamboocraftmc.net

1. Who We Are

BambooCraft is a community-run Minecraft server and related services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Minecraft username and UUID
  • IP address (linked to usernames upon login)
  • Chat and command logs
  • Gameplay activity logs
  • Discord IDs (if linked)
  • Payments handled by Tebex (we do not store payment data)

3. How We Use Data

  • Moderation and rule enforcement
  • Security, including preventing ban evasion
  • Server operation and administration

4. Data Sharing

We may share relevant data (including IP addresses and associated account information) with trusted partners for moderation, security, and operational purposes.

  • Discord (per their policies)
  • Tebex (store transactions)
  • Server partners or affiliated communities for enforcement of bans and abuse prevention

5. Data Retention

Logs, IP data, and account associations may be stored indefinitely for moderation and security purposes.

6. Children’s Privacy

Players under 13 should have parental permission to use the server.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time.
